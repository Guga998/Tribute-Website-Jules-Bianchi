Jules Bianchi was a talented French Formula 1 driver who was born in 1989. He started his racing career in karting before moving up to single-seater championships. In 2013, he made his Formula 1 debut with the Marussia team. Bianchi quickly showed his talent and scored his first Formula 1 points at the 2014 Monaco Grand Prix. Many people believed that he had the potential to become one of the sport’s future stars. However, his career was tragically interrupted by a serious accident at the 2014 Japanese Grand Prix. After months in hospital, Jules Bianchi sadly passed away in July 2015 at the age of 25. He is still remembered as a talented and promising driver who left a lasting mark on Formula 1.
